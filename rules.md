# Dungeon! (2014 Wizards of the Coast edition) — rules summary

> The sim's own summary and notes, written in our words. When it differs from the official rulebook, the rulebook wins.
1–8 players.

## Components

- Board: 6 levels plus the Great Hall (a safe chamber in the center, start and finish)
  - Level colors: 1 yellow, 2 orange, 3 red, 4 purple, 5 blue, 6 green
  - Rough layout: L1 in the center; L2 west; L3 NW and SW; L4 N and S; L5 NE and SE; L6 east
  - Board edge has a monster-card slot track numbered 1–12 and a "Monster Fights Back" table
- 8 hero standees: 2 Rogues (green), 2 Clerics (blue), 2 Fighters (red), 2 Wizards (purple)
- 165 cards: 61 Monster, 80 Treasure, 24 Spell
- 139 tokens: 12 Number (1–12), 11 Lose a Turn, 111 Defeated, 5 Magic Sword (+1/+2)
- 2d6

## Heroes

| Class   | Treasure to win | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| Rogue   | 10,000 gp | finds secret doors on 3–6 (others 5–6); "safest on L1–3" |
| Cleric  | 10,000 gp | "safest on L2–4" |
| Fighter | 20,000 gp | best at regular combat; "enjoys L3–5" |
| Wizard  | 30,000 gp | only class that casts Spells; cannot use Magic Swords; "good on L4–6" |

Each class has two named characters; the names are cosmetic.

## Spaces

- **Room**: a colored square; hides 1 Monster guarding 1 Treasure
- **Chamber**: a large space; hides 3 Monsters, no Treasure
- **Hallway**: no monsters
- Each room or chamber (including the Great Hall) counts as 1 space
- Rooms and chambers can be entered only through a door; doors don't count as spaces
- **Secret doors** are impassable until found (see Movement)

## Board facts

- Hallways are drawn as flagstone tiles, and each tile is one movement space
- 12 named chambers:
  - L2: Kitchen, Guard Room (north)
  - L3: Armory (NW), Pantry (NE)
  - L4: Cells (W), Chapel (E)
  - L5: The Hole, Torture Chamber (SW), Laboratory, Crypt (SE)
  - L6: The Lair, The Burrow (S-center)
- Great Hall (L1) is in the center, with yellow L1 rooms ringed around it
- Board's own summary table agrees with the rulebook: Rogue finds secret doors on 3–6, everyone else on 5–6. "Safest levels" are Rogue 1–3, Cleric 2–4, Fighter 3–5, Wizard 4–6
- Board's fight-back table: Seriously Hurt drops half the treasure "round up"; Hurt means "retreat one space"
- Door icon is a wooden door; secret door icon is a white bar across the hallway

## Setup

1. Sort the Monster and Treasure cards into 6 piles each by level (by card back) and shuffle each pile
2. Wizards build their spellbooks: roll 1d6 + 6, which gives 7–12 spells, and choose any mix of Fireball, Lightning Bolt, and Teleport. With 2 Wizards, alternate picks one card at a time
3. All heroes start in the Great Hall. Each player rolls 2d6, highest goes first, reroll ties, and play passes left

## Turn

If you have any Lose a Turn tokens, discard 1 at the start of your turn and your turn ends.

### Step 1: Move

- Move up to 5 spaces; no die roll is needed, and you may choose not to move
- You can move through other heroes but can't stop on another hero's space, except in a chamber
- If you start your turn in a room with a Monster, you may leave
- Ending in a hallway, a room with 1 Defeated token, or a chamber with 3 Defeated tokens means no encounter, and your turn ends
- Entering a room with no Defeated token, or a chamber with fewer than 3, means you stop and go to Step 2

**Secret doors:** move into the space next to the door. If you still have at least 1 move left, roll 1d6: 5–6 finds the door (Rogue 3–6). On success you may keep moving, and the door is open to *you* for the rest of the game. On failure you must stop.

### Step 2: Encounter

- If the space has a **Number token**, the monster in the matching slot is waiting there; fight it
- Otherwise draw from that level's Monster pile and place the card face up. If a level's pile runs out, reshuffle that level's discards
- In a chamber only 1 of its 3 monsters appears at a time
- If the card is a **Trap**, follow it instead of fighting (see Traps)

### Step 3: Combat

**Your attack:** roll 2d6 and add any Magic Sword bonus. Each Monster card shows 6 target numbers, one under each icon: Rogue, Cleric, Fighter, Wizard, Fireball, and Lightning Bolt. Meeting or beating your icon's number defeats it.

- "–" under your icon means you can't attack it unless you have a Magic Sword, which treats the "–" as 13
- **Win:** discard the monster card, put a Defeated token in the space, and go to Step 4
- **Miss:** the monster fights back. Roll 2d6:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| 12   | **Crushed!** Drop all Treasure cards. Your hero leaves the board; choose a new hero (or the same one if no other is free) and place it in the Great Hall |
| 11   | **Seriously Hurt:** drop half your Treasure cards at random (board says round up) and put your hero in the Great Hall |
| 8–10 | **Hurt:** drop 1 random Treasure card, move back 1 space in the direction you came, and take 1 Lose a Turn token |
| 6–7  | **Stunned:** drop 1 random Treasure card |
| ≤5   | **Miss!** No effect |

In every fight-back case the Monster card goes to the first open number slot (1–12), the matching Number token goes in the room or chamber, and your turn ends.

**Dropping treasure:** dropped cards go face up under the card of the monster that hit you, so they stay in that space for whoever defeats it. Random drops are made by shuffling your cards and discarding blind.

The monster's attack table is the same for every monster; only your to-hit number depends on the monster.

### Step 4: Loot (only if you defeated a monster this turn)

- **Room:** draw 1 Treasure card of the room's level and also collect any treasures dropped there
- **Chamber:** no Treasure draw; collect only dropped treasures

### Traps

Traps are Monster cards. On drawing one:

- In a room you also draw 1 Treasure card of the room's level; in a chamber you get no treasure
- Place 1 Defeated token in the space, then follow the card
- Examples:
  - **Cage Trap:** gain 1 treasure, then roll 1d6: 1–3 lose 1 turn, 4–6 lose 2 turns
  - **Slide Trap:** gain 1 treasure and move to any chamber on level 4 (the level printed on the card). If that chamber isn't fully cleared, run Step 2 again

### Winning

Once you hold enough treasure for your class, end your turn in the Great Hall and you win.

## Special treasure cards

- **Secret Door Card (500 gp):** you pass all secret doors without rolling. If you drop it, you keep the doors you've already found
- **ESP Medallion (500 gp):** while next to a room or chamber, draw that level's Monster card to see what's inside. You can do this mid-move; if you then choose not to enter, your turn ends
- **Crystal Ball (2,000 gp):** instead of moving and encountering, pick any room or chamber on the board and draw its level's Monster card (plus a Treasure card if it's a room)
- For both ESP and Crystal Ball, the drawn cards stay face down in the first open number slot and the Number token goes in the space; they're revealed when a hero next enters. You can use one ESP-or-Crystal-Ball power per turn, however many you hold
- **Magic Sword (500 gp; Wizards can't use it):** on pickup roll 2d6 against the table printed on that card. The table varies by card: deeper swords are better and worth more gp (see `treasures.csv`)

  | Sword | +1 on | +2 on | P(+2) | gp |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | L1 | 2–11 | 12 | 3% | 500 |
  | L2 | 2–9 | 10–12 | 17% | 500 |
  | L4 ×2 | 2–8 | 9–12 | 28% | 1,000 |
  | L6 | 2–6 | 7–12 | 58% | 2,000 |

- Special-card gp also varies by level: ESP Medallion is 500 on L3 and 1,500 on L5, and Crystal Ball is 1,000 on L4 and 2,000 on L6 Take the matching token; the bonus adds to every attack roll. "–" counts as 13
  - Only 1 sword can be in play; extra swords are just treasure (they still count toward winning)
  - If a random drop takes your active sword, you may immediately switch to a spare and keep the same bonus token
  - A Wizard holding a sword only counts its gp

## Wizard spells

You may cast 1 spell per turn; cast cards are discarded.

- **Teleport:** only while in a chamber and before Step 3. Move to another chamber on the same level, one level lower, or one level higher. You don't fight the monster in the chamber you leave, but you must fight any monster in the chamber you arrive at
- **Fireball / Lightning Bolt:** if you have enough movement left to enter a room or chamber, stop at its door or in the space next to the chamber and declare the spell
  - If the monster isn't revealed yet, you must pick which spell **before** drawing the card
  - Roll against the number under that spell's icon
  - On a hit, move in and loot as normal
  - On a miss, the monster does **not** fight back (you aren't in its space) and stays there until defeated
- **Regaining spells:** end your turn in the Great Hall. On your next turn roll 1d6, take up to that many Spell cards back into your hand, and end your turn. You can never hold more than your starting count

## Solo variants

- **Treasure Hunt:** draw a Level 1 Treasure card, shuffle it back, and find that treasure before being Crushed twice. Next time try a higher-level treasure
- **Timed Game:** win within 30 minutes
- **Become the Hunted:** a face-down Level 6 monster starts in a Level 6 room, using a spare standee
  - It moves 5 spaces toward you after each of your turns by the shortest path; it can open secret doors and doesn't stop in rooms
  - If it reaches your space, it's revealed and attacks; a miss means it fights back as normal
  - You can attack it like any monster; if you defeat it, a new Level 6 hunter spawns

## Open questions and ambiguities for the sim

- Rulebook p.3 labels the yellow-bolt spell card "Fireball" and the orange-swirl card "Lightning Bolt". The monster-card callout and p.8 give the reverse, which looks right: Fireball = orange swirl, Lightning = yellow bolt
- Spell deck is 9 Fireball, 9 Lightning Bolt, and 6 Teleport (counted from the box). A Wizard picks freely from what remains. Two Wizards with 12-spell books would use all 24 cards, so the sim must enforce these counts
- Are hallways divided into discrete spaces? The movement illustration shows circles along the path. We need the board close-ups
- Hurt: "move back 1 space the way you came" when you came from the Great Hall or arrived by Teleport or Slide
- Does a Crushed Wizard rebuild the spellbook?
- Are found secret doors per hero, and does that knowledge survive being Crushed?
